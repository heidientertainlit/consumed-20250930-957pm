Media reviews/takes must not require a separate title field.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the review form has no title part and they do not want one.

**How to apply:** Improve scannability through presentation of the existing take text, not by adding a title-writing step or a generated headline. Use a darker, semibold natural opening sentence within longer takes, with lighter continuation. Short single-sentence takes should also be semibold: the user explicitly asked for “Season one was better” to be bold after seeing the first pass. Do not invent or extract a separate headline.
