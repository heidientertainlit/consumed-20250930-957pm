Keep the purple media header ordered as poster/details/providers (where to watch/read/listen), Rate and the user's current list status (or “Add to my list”), description, then More like this. Put the subtle rating/list controls side by side in one shared row below the poster/details block, above the description—not stacked in the narrow right-hand info column. The white area begins with Takes/Play tabs.

**Why:** After seeing the controls stacked beside the poster, the user asked to keep them minimal but place both side by side on the lower row where Finished was. More like this belongs in the purple area like the DNA page.

**How to apply:** Preserve this ordering during later media-page changes. Keep starter prompts editable and conversations visually threaded; preserve Consumed's thumbs-up/thumbs-down, reply, report, and share actions rather than adopting the hearts shown in the references.

The media-detail social section uses rounded Takes/Play segments with a purple selected tab. The Takes view starts with “What people are saying,” a real take count, and a compact avatar / “Add your take…” input with a purple send icon, not a plus button.

**Why:** The user supplied this specific reference and wants the control to communicate posting rather than adding something to a list. They also requested the same rounded, purple-selected tab treatment for DNA / My Media on their own profile.

**How to apply:** Keep the default input compact and submit actual takes through the existing posting flow. Preserve optional rating and editable starter prompts in the expanded composer, rather than putting the old “Start a conversation” panel above the take list.

Show the media type explicitly and prominently near the title on every media-detail page.

**Why:** The user showed a book detail page whose cover and “by” line were not enough to make its type clear.

**How to apply:** Keep the type visible at a glance for books and other media without depending on the poster, creator wording, runtime, or provider chips to communicate it. The user's preferred presentation is a lavender outline icon with an uppercase lavender type label directly on the dark header (movie clapperboard, book icon), not white text inside a purple pill. Keep the icon and label visually smaller than the media title.

Media reviews/takes must not require a separate title field.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the review form has no title part and they do not want one.

**How to apply:** Improve scannability through presentation of the existing take text, not by adding a title-writing step or a generated headline. Use a darker, semibold natural opening sentence within longer takes, with lighter continuation. Short single-sentence takes should also be semibold: the user explicitly asked for “Season one was better” to be bold after seeing the first pass. Do not invent or extract a separate headline.
