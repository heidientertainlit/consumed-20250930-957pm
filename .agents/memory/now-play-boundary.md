---
name: Now and Play boundary
description: Product boundary between the social Now feed and full interactive Play experiences.
---

Full playable Trivia, Cast Your Vote, Debate the Rank, and Seen It feeds belong under the Play page's mode tabs. Do not reintroduce these complete game modules into Now.

**Why:** Now is primarily for social activity and Play-generated stories. Mounting full game feeds there duplicates Play, makes the social feed noisy, and causes inactive game data work.

**How to apply:** New playable modes should live in Play. Now may later receive lightweight social teaser cards about friends' Play activity, but those teasers must link into Play rather than embed the full experience.
