---
name: Rated feed card structure
description: Approved hierarchy for rated and reviewed posts in the main feed.
---

Keep the poster, title, stars, match score, short review, and reviewer identity together as the primary media moment. Once comments exist, present conversation context beneath it as a distinct lighter layer with participant avatars and a concise “people talking” label. Do not show an empty conversation panel before the first comment; the Reply action starts the thread.

**Why:** The user approved this separation because the compact rate card stays easy to scan while the conversation layer makes replies more prominent without visually overwhelming the media.

**How to apply:** Preserve both thumbs, Reply (including its bubble icon), Rate, Share, post reporting, comment reporting, and threaded replies when changing rated feed cards. Never collapse those capabilities into decorative or nonfunctional controls.
