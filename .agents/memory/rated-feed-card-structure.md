name: Rated feed card structure
description: Approved hierarchy for rated and reviewed posts in the main feed.
---

Keep the poster, title, stars, match score, short review, and reviewer identity together as the primary media moment. A rating and its review text may arrive as separate rating-only and thought records; pair same-user/same-media companions created close together so stars stay with the visible review, and suppress the redundant rating-only slide. Once comments exist, present conversation context beneath it as a distinct lighter layer.

**Why:** The user approved this separation because the compact rate card stays easy to scan while the conversation layer makes replies more prominent without visually overwhelming the media. Rendering companion records independently made review cards appear to lose their stars.

**How to apply:** Preserve both thumbs, Reply (including its bubble icon), Rate, Share, post reporting, comment reporting, and threaded replies when changing rated feed cards. Standalone rated/list-activity cards must use the same grouped thumbs-up/thumbs-down treatment as the main card path rather than the legacy heart footer. Never collapse those capabilities into decorative or nonfunctional controls. Reply previews must be readable in guest mode without exposing private profile fields. Once the comment request succeeds, the actual flattened comment tree is authoritative over denormalized counts: show a real reply preview when present, and hide stale reply counts when no comment row exists.

The featured “What People Are Saying” heading belongs inside its carousel's white card in the app's established media-title serif style. Omit the “Real ratings and reviews from your feed” subtitle.

**Why:** The user requested the internal placement and serif heading, then corrected that a separate heavier Fraunces setting was not the style the app uses.

**How to apply:** Match the existing media-title serif family, medium weight, tight leading and letter spacing rather than selecting a different font solely by name. Keep this heading specific to the featured Now review carousel.

For the featured carousel, put avatar and shortened reviewer name first, then commentary at its original size, then the smaller Fraunces title and season. Commentary may be clamped. Shorten multi-surname display names using the first surname's initial, even for the owner or accepted friends.

**Why:** The user requested the short identity above the quote, then revised the layout to restore the commentary size and place the smaller Fraunces title and season below it.

**How to apply:** Keep this localized to featured reviews; preserve stars, match pills, related ratings, all reaction controls, and owner delete.

Use a subtle overlapping-card edge plus a simple upper-right “>” to suggest more reviews, without dots, “Next” labels, thumbnails, or a previous/next navigation strip. Keep the Fraunces heading slightly smaller than Trending now.

**Why:** The user rejected the added next-review strip and preview, then specifically requested an upper-right “>” and a heading slightly smaller than Trending now.

**How to apply:** Preserve swipe navigation and show the overlap only when the carousel contains multiple posts. The corner chevron advances to another review and must not overlap reporting. Do not introduce another labeled navigation strip.
