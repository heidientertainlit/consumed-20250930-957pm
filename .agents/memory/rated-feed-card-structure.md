---
name: Rated feed card structure
description: Approved hierarchy for rated and reviewed posts in the main feed.
---

Keep the poster, title, stars, match score, short review, and reviewer identity together as the primary media moment. Present conversation context beneath it as a distinct lighter layer with participant avatars, reply count, and an invitation to join.

**Why:** The user approved this separation because the compact rate card stays easy to scan while the conversation layer makes replies more prominent without visually overwhelming the media.

**How to apply:** Preserve both thumbs, Reply, Rate, Share, post reporting, comment reporting, and threaded replies when changing rated feed cards. Never collapse those capabilities into decorative or nonfunctional controls.
