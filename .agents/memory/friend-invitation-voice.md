---
name: Friend invitation voice
description: Preferred messaging direction for the future Find & invite friends experience.
---

Friend invitations should lead with curiosity about comparing Entertainment DNA and discovering the pair's match, rather than generic referral or growth language.

**Why:** The product-specific reason to join is more compelling and personal than “Join this app.”

**How to apply:** Use concise prompts in the spirit of “How similar is our Entertainment DNA? Join me on Consumed and let's compare.” or “Okay, I need to know our Entertainment DNA match.” Preserve the inviter’s natural voice and avoid overexplaining.
