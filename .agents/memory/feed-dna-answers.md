---
name: Feed DNA answers
description: How feed DNA Moment answers contribute to Entertainment DNA and when their cards should disappear.
---

Treat each feed DNA Moment choice as explicit, self-reported entertainment-personality evidence, not merely an engagement count or broad media-category interaction.

**Why:** Options such as preferring solo viewing versus group watch parties describe how someone experiences entertainment. Counting only that a question was answered discards the meaning the user intentionally supplied.

**How to apply:** Persist answers through the authenticated server path, translate stored option keys back to their human-readable question and answer, and include that evidence in DNA signal/profile refreshes without replacing stronger ratings or survey data. After every answer, refresh all feed-question slots; once no active feed questions remain unanswered, show no DNA Moment cards.
