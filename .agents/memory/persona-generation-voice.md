---
name: Persona generation voice
description: User intent for natural variation without quotas or template-generated personas
---

For persona content, the user prioritizes “Natural > mathematically perfect diversity.”
Modes describe behaviors, not fixed phrases or templates. Individual voices should be
tendencies rather than caricatures. Plain, tiny, low-energy and rating-only reactions
are valid alongside longer considered reactions.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose natural variation over perfectly balanced outputs.

**How to apply:** When extending persona generation, preserve existing identities and
use probabilistic variation rather than fixed mode rotations, mandatory cleverness or
exact per-batch diversity quotas.
