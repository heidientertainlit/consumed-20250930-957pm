---
name: Content Research editorial boundary
description: Admin title-based research must remain separate from personal DNA and publishing.
---
Content Research is editorial research about a selected title, not a user's
personal DNA prediction. Keep research drafts session-only, and preserve honest
partial coverage rather than forcing a result for every medium.

**Why:** The user explicitly scoped this admin tool to curated cross-media copy,
without personal recommendation changes, posting, scheduling, or another catalog.

**How to apply:** Reuse catalog integrations, but never route research through
personal recommendation/rebuild endpoints. Catalog identity proves the title
exists; it does not prove a subjective connection or an official relationship.
