---
name: YouTube leaderboard scoring
description: Product decision for how tracked YouTube items affect leaderboard categories and overall ranking.
---

Treat YouTube as a first-class consumption leaderboard category and include its points in the overall ranking, rather than showing a category-only board.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose full leaderboard participation. The intended weight is 2 points per tracked YouTube item, positioned between Music (1 point) and Podcasts (3 points).

**How to apply:** Any leaderboard or user-points calculation that changes media scoring must keep the YouTube category and overall-rank contribution in sync.
