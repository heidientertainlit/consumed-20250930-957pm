Render DNA shares directly to a high-resolution portrait canvas and keep the complete profile paragraph. Do not replace the paragraph with abbreviated copy, and do not use a DOM screenshot renderer for exported or shared images.

**Why:** The full paragraph is required content. DOM screenshotting duplicated and overlapped text on iOS, while the direct-canvas version was confirmed as the right visual approach.

**How to apply:** Use explicit canvas text wrapping for both Share and Save to Photos. Keep the recognizable multi-turn double helix with base-pair crossbars; avoid a single wide loop that resembles an ichthys.
