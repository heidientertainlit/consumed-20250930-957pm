---
name: Automatic affinity discovery
description: Privacy and ordering rule for People affinity discovery.
---

Eligible non-private profiles participate in affinity discovery automatically. Show accepted friends first, followed by new people with similar Entertainment DNA. Continue excluding blocked relationships, personas, private DNA profiles, and accounts without enough tracked items.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose automatic discovery so People does not remain friend-only while waiting for individual opt-ins.

**How to apply:** Do not restore an affinity opt-in toggle or gate non-friend candidates on a discoverability preference. Preserve private-profile and block protections.
