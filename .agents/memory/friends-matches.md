---
name: Friends and matches
description: Product rule for presenting affinity discovery and established friendships in People.
---

Matches and Friends belong in one Friends destination. Lead with a small number of premium, poster-rich high matches, then provide relationship management and compact additional discovery. Keep accepted friends out of the match-discovery lists so people are not duplicated.

**Why:** Matching is how users discover potential friends, not a separate long-term relationship type. Separate top-level tabs made People feel crowded and split one journey across two destinations.

**How to apply:** Keep People navigation to Friends, Tribes, and Creators. The Friends experience must retain search, invites, incoming-request actions, pending states, current friends, and affinity discovery. Legacy Matches links should resolve to Friends.
