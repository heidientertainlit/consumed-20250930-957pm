---
name: Play friend challenges
description: Product rules for sharing trivia and Daily Play to compare results with a friend.
---

Friend challenges must share the exact trivia or Daily Play item and show the sender-versus-recipient result inside the existing result card. Keep the challenge action and comparison within the current mobile viewport; do not add a separate comparison page or require vertical scrolling.

**Why:** The intended experience is a fast one-screen handoff: share the exact game, let the friend answer, then compare both outcomes in place.

**How to apply:** Pin shared trivia first, preserve previously answered results, replace existing footer/social-proof space with the compact comparison, and never expose a comparison link until the sender's answer is durably saved.
