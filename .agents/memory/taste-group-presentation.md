---
name: Taste group presentation
description: Product rule for presenting Tribe clusters to users without manufactured identities.
---

Present Tribe clusters as unnamed taste groups. Lead with recognizable media covers, simple media types, member previews, and match strength; keep generated names and abstract evidence labels out of the user-facing identity.

**Why:** Generated labels can misrepresent a mixed taste cluster and use language that does not mean much to a typical user. Concrete media and people make the connection understandable and credible.

**How to apply:** Backend names and stable IDs may remain for clustering and routing, but list cards, detail views, joining, sharing, and explanatory copy should describe a taste group rather than expose a named Tribe.
