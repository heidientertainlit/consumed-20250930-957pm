---
name: Taste group presentation
description: Product rule for presenting Tribe clusters to users without manufactured identities.
---

Present Tribe clusters as unnamed groups beneath the original Tribes section heading. Lead with recognizable media covers, simple media types, member previews, and match percentage; keep generated names and abstract evidence labels out of the user-facing identity. Only the highest-scoring mixed cross-media group is called “People who have similar taste overall.” Other groups use “People who like the same [category] you do,” based on actual dominant media mix or strongest genre evidence, including YouTube and podcasts.

**Why:** Generated labels can misrepresent a mixed taste cluster and use language that does not mean much to a typical user. Concrete media and people make the connection understandable and credible.

**How to apply:** Backend names and stable IDs may remain for clustering and routing, but cards and detail views describe the concrete connection rather than expose a generated name. Show only “N% match” as plain text—no qualitative levels or pill.
