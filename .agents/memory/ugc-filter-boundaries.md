---
name: UGC filter boundaries
description: Preserve ordinary updates and abuse-report evidence when extending content screening.
---

Validate new or changed public user-authored text, not unchanged legacy text during unrelated updates. Keep abuse reports and support feedback available for verbatim evidence.

**Why:** Revalidating entire rows can break likes, counters, or status changes because of old content. Filtering support submissions can prevent users from reporting the very abuse moderation needs to examine.

**How to apply:** Test unchanged legacy text with non-text updates, direct user and service writes, and nested user-authored options. Exempt actual catalog metadata deliberately, not whole mixed-purpose tables. Treat conservative term screening as one precaution, not a replacement for functioning reports and human moderation.
