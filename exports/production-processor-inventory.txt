Status vocabulary used below
----------------------------
YES = directly established by current deployed source or live read-only
configuration. NO = explicit source/configuration says it is not sent or not
enabled. UNABLE = source does not establish the provider-side behavior or an
exact runtime field set. "No account ID in request" does not mean anonymous:
content can still be identifying, transport metadata and provider logs are
outside the request body, and server-side linkage may be possible.
